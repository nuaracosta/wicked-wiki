Madame Morrible

Madame Morrible is present when a new group of students arrives at Shiz University. She is one of the witnesses as Elphaba Thropp loses her temper after a teacher's mistreatment of her sister, Nessarose and emits a burst of magic. She covers for Elphaba Thropp, claiming that she was actually responsible for the magic. When she learns that Elphaba Thropp is not actually a new student at the university, she offers to take her on as her own personal protégé and teach her how to control her magic. This greatly upsets Galinda, who was hoping herself to learn magic. To add insult to injury, as Elphaba Thropp does not yet have a room at Shiz University, Morrible declares that Elphaba Thropp is to room with Galinda.
