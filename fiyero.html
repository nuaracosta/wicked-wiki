Fyiero Tigelaar

Fyiero Tigelaar is the free spirited prince of the Arjiki tribe, from Winkie Country. After being repeatedly expelled from previous schools for his rebellious and improper behavior, Fiyero finds his way to Shiz University, enrolling well after most students. The night he arrives, he stumbles across a peculiar girl with green skin, Elphaba Thropp, nearly crashing into her with his horse. Fiyero quickly apologizes, but is left momentarily speechless by Elphaba. After they take the lion cub from the cage it had been placed, they develop feelings for each other, in spite of Fyiero dating Glinda.
