Magic

Magic> is a mystical power which lives in the magical land of Oz, although it is a land overflowing with magic, there is a small group of beings who are capable of using it as their own, the best known being the witches and fairies. It is implied that there are people who are born with the ability to do magic, although those who are not born with it are capable of acquiring it through years of study and learning, although their magic is not as strong as that of someone born with magic. Elphaba has innate powers, whereas Madame Morrible had to study for years to read the Grimmerie.
