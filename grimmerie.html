Grimmerie

The Grimmerie is an ancient book of spells, left by the original founders of Oz. It is said to be difficult to read, with Morrible taking years to be able to read a few words. There is a prophecy that the person to free Oz from its current dark times would be able to read it. The Wizard is believed to have read it, but later on we learn that he was just saying his hometown name. Elphaba, however, has the Grimmerie open to her in her first experience with it. Spells cast through the Grimmerie cannot be reversed.
