Oz Politics

The Land of Oz was once a very magical and harmonized place, until the Great Drought happened and animals started being blamed for it. It is unknown how the Wizard became the dictator he did, however, he mentions to Elphaba how people just needed something to hate to come together. Along with Morrible, he then becomes a dictator and not only scapegoats Elphaba into being Wicked, but spreads fear in the land for the name of unity. It is also important to point out that the Wizard has no magical powers, only genius inventions that mimics magic. He is only a con artist.
